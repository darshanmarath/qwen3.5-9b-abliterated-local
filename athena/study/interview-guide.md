# Interview guide

20 minutes. Record only with spoken consent at the start.

**Opening (2 min)**
- Thank them. Repeat: voluntary, can stop any time, quotes not attributed.
- "Is it all right if I record this for my notes?"

**Their last study (6 min)**
- "Tell me about the last piece of research you ran, from start to finish."
- "Which part did you put off?"
- "Which part would you never skip?"

**Handing work over (8 min)**
- "In the survey you said you'd let an agent {task} but not {task}. Walk me
  through the difference."
- "Think of a time you used an AI tool for research. What happened?"
- "What would an agent have to show you before you trusted its themes?"
- "If it drafted an email to a participant, what would you check first?"

**Where the model runs (2 min)**
- "Does it matter to you where the participant data goes? Why?"

**Close (2 min)**
- "What did I not ask that I should have?"
- Thank them. Repeat how to withdraw.
