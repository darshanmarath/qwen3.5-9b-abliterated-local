**About this study**

I'm Darshan Manoj, a product designer in Berlin. I'm studying which parts of
user research designers would hand to an AI agent, and where they want to
stay in control. This is a self-initiated project.

**What taking part involves**

A survey of about five minutes. If you agree at the end, I may ask you for a
20-minute follow-up call.

**What I collect and how I use it**

Your answers, and your name and email if you give them. I use them only for
this study. Your answers are stored on my own computer under a participant
number, not your name. An AI model that runs on that computer helps me sort
the answers. I do not upload your answers to any online AI service.

I may publish findings and short quotes in a portfolio case study. Quotes
are never attributed by name.

**Your choices**

Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question. You can withdraw at any
time by emailing darshanmarath@gmail.com, and I will delete your answers.
I delete names and emails when the study closes.
