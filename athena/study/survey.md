# Survey

About five minutes. Consent text first (see `consent.md`).

1. **Consent.** I have read the information above and agree to take part.
   (Yes / No. "No" ends the survey.)

2. **In the last twelve months, which of these have you done yourself?**
   (Tick all that apply.)
   - Recruited participants
   - Written a survey or interview guide
   - Run interviews or usability tests
   - Analysed responses or transcripts
   - Presented findings
   - None of these

3. **Think of your most recent piece of research. Which part took the most
   time?** (Open text.)

4. **For each task, what would you let an AI agent do?**
   (Do it alone / Draft it for me to approve / Not at all / I don't do this)
   - Find and list people to invite
   - Write invite and reminder emails
   - Send those emails
   - Write survey or interview questions
   - Transcribe and tidy interview notes
   - Group answers into themes
   - Pick the quotes that go in the report
   - Write the findings

5. **Pick one task you answered "Not at all" for. What makes you keep it?**
   (Open text. Skip if none.)

6. **Have you used an AI tool for any research task in the last twelve
   months?** (Yes / No)

7. **If yes: what did you use it for, and what happened?** (Open text.)

8. **An AI research tool can run on a company's servers or on your own
   computer. Would that change what you'd give it?**
   (Yes / No / Not sure) — **Why?** (Open text.)

9. **About you.** Your role, and years of experience. (Role: open text.
   Years: 0–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / 10+)

10. **May I contact you for a 20-minute follow-up call?**
    (Yes, with email / No)
